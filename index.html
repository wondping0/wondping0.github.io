How the flags fell

Full solvers from past competitions, mostly cryptography.

  • National Cyber Week 2023 Quals Recovering an AES-CFB8 key, a man-in-the-middle on Diffie–Hellman and a low-exponent attack on a double RSA scheme. 3 solves →
  • TCP1P CTF 2023 RSA leaks via gcd, meet-in-the-middle on double AES, Mersenne Twister prediction, LFSR mask recovery, a CBC padding oracle and small-e RSA behind an ECC captcha. 7 solves →
